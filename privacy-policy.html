Pop Pals - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Pop Pals is a game for children and families. We do not collect, store, share or sell any personal information.

  • No account or sign-in is required.
  • The game contains no ads, no analytics and no third-party SDKs.
  • There are no in-app purchases with real money. Coins are earned only by playing.
  • Game progress (coins, unlocked items, settings) is stored only on your device and is never sent anywhere.
